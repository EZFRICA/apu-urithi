# 0014: Anyone who has knowledge teaches; the guardian's side grows, the learner's stays a tutor

Decided: 2026-10-07, by the owner.

## Context
A brainstorm on where Urithi goes after Step 6: the guardian's side becomes a pupil who is
expert in teaching (it notes what it is told to, and asks only when a detail it judges
important for learning is missing, a question the guardian may pass), with the switches in
the guardian's hands (docs/decisions/0013). Four questions were left open, answered here.

## Decision
1. **Who teaches:** anyone who has knowledge to share, not one kind of organisation.
2. **The learner's side does not take the expert's initiative.** It stays a tutor that answers
   when asked, from the guardian's words, as it does now; it does not step in on its own.
3. **No learners live in the class, for now.** The camera already covers the case: a guardian
   films themselves teaching, and what is captured is then made available online.
4. **The same control everywhere.** On both sides, nothing listens, records or reads aloud
   unless the person has asked for it (a switch, a button held, a box ticked).

## Consequences
- The next work is on the guardian's side: commands understood by intent, questions judged
  by importance and skippable, the camera's own rules.
- Checked on the learner's page: its microphone opens only while a button is held, and it
  reads aloud only on a click, a key, or "no screen" ticked by the learner.
- "Made available online" from a filmed class: whether the recording itself is published,
  or only the knowledge captured from it, is to be decided with video sources (0009).

## Alternatives set aside
- A learner's tutor that steps in by itself when it judges a gap important.
- Learners joining the class live (a real-time multi-person project of its own).
