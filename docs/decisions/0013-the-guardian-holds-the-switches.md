# 0013: The guardian holds the switches; Urithi never listens on its own

Decided: 2026-10-07, by the owner.

## Context
Thinking about where the classroom goes next, the owner compared it with the notes and the
transcription of a video meeting, which hear everything. Urithi is to be a pupil who is
expert in teaching: it notes what it is told to, and asks only when a detail it judges
important for learning is missing, a question the guardian may pass. The same holds for the
camera. Until then, the class opened the microphone by itself, to tell pauses from speech,
and again after each question, to hear the answer.

## Decision
Urithi does not transcribe continuously. It listens only when it awaits an answer, and only
while the guardian has turned the microphone on. The guardian alone turns on and off the
microphone, listening after a question, and the vision (screen or camera). The microphone
is off until they turn it on; the switches are kept for their next class.

## Consequences
- With the microphone off, pauses are read from the screen alone, and an answer is typed.
- No feature may open the microphone or start a capture without the guardian's switch.

## Alternatives set aside
- A continuous transcript with notes, as a meeting tool: not what the guardian asked for.
