# 0003: Prerequisites are reminded, not enforced

Decided: 2026-10-05, by the owner.

## Context
A knowledge needs an order, or the learner drowns; a learner who already knows a part must not be held back.

## Decision
When a part, an answer or an exam case rests on an unlearned prerequisite, the learner is told and offered to go there first, and may carry on.

## Consequences
The learner's contents show each part's state; the tutor names missing prerequisites.

## Alternatives set aside
- Blocking: holds back people who revise.
- Per guardian choice: more settings than needed now.
