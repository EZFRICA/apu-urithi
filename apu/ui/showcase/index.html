Urithi · Swahili for inheritance

What a person knows, handed on in their own words.

A guardian teaches by talking, typing or showing their screen. Urithi asks why at the right moment, writes the rules down in their words, tests them, and publishes nothing until they have heard it back. Learners then learn the way they need: reading, listening, braille, practising, asking, online or not.

How a knowledge travels

  1. 01 · TeachTalk, type or show your screen A teleprompter shows every word as it is heard.
  2. 02 · StructureUrithi asks why, then tests you Your rules in your words, the cases where they are least clear, checked against public sources.
  3. 03 · PublishHeard back, then handed on Each part at the level you choose: public, community or restricted.
  4. 04 · LearnRead, listen, braille, practise, ask On a path, with reviews when they are due. Answers quote the guardian or say nothing.

What is inside

The guardian's working memory What Urithi is in the middle of The question it asked, the cases still to test, what it retained. Kept, so teaching carries on after a restart.
The library What the guardian validated and published In the guardian's own words, each part at its access level, filtered inside the search, organisation by organisation.
The learner's session Where they were, what they asked, what was hard Found again on another device. Never read by the guardian's side.
The learner's progress Reviews when they are due, and a notebook Spaced reviews (FSRS), the exam marked by code against the guardian's decisions, a revision sheet on screen or in braille.
The guard Every question is read before anything costly runs: on the topic, off it, or distress. Distress gets a caring reply, and is never stopped by a quota nor stored.
Braille Each part, an answer, a revision sheet: grade 1 or 2, forty cells a line, ready for a display or an embosser.
Offline A signed package of what a learner may see; reading, listening, braille, practice and the exam work with no network.
Sources A PDF by its page, a recording by its moment: a step cites where it comes from, and a learner can open it.
The cost, held Quotas per person, ceilings per organisation; near a ceiling, a cached answer or the guardian's own words, before a refusal.
The classroom Share a screen or a camera; Urithi asks why at the pauses, and what it captures waits for the guardian to validate it.
Faithful, checked by code Every sentence of an answer cites the guardian; every quote is verbatim. Otherwise their own words are shown.
For every way of learning Keyboard first, screen readers, braille sheets and embossers, voice in and out, English and French.
Offline, signed Keep a knowledge on your device. A changed word, answer or date is refused, with no network.
